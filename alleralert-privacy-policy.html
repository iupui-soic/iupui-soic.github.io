Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us

Last Updated: December 29, 2025

Introduction

AllerAlert ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile health application designed for proactive asthma management.

By using AllerAlert, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access the application.

Information We Collect

Personal Information

We may collect personally identifiable information that you voluntarily provide when using AllerAlert, including:

  • Name and email address (via email registration, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In)
  • Profile information from your authentication provider
  • Health-related data you choose to log (symptoms, triggers, medications)

Environmental Data

When connected to an Atmotube PRO device via Bluetooth, we collect:

  • VOCs (Volatile Organic Compounds) readings
  • Particulate Matter levels (PM1, PM2.5, PM10)
  • Temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure

Location Information

With your permission, we may collect location data to:

  • Provide hyper-local weather forecasts
  • Correlate environmental conditions with your symptoms
  • Identify environmental risk zones

Voice Data

When using voice-enabled symptom tracking, your voice input is processed to extract symptoms, triggers, and medication information. Voice recordings are processed in real-time and are not stored permanently.

How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the AllerAlert service
  • Generate personalized health insights and trend visualizations
  • Analyze environmental patterns and identify triggers
  • Improve our app's features and user experience
  • Send important notifications about your health data
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data Storage and Security

Important: All user data is stored securely using Firebase services. We implement industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect your personal health information.
  • Firebase Authentication: Secure user authentication via email, Google Sign-In, or Apple Sign-In
  • Cloud Firestore: Encrypted, real-time cloud database for your health data
  • No PHI Storage: No Protected Health Information (PHI) is stored outside of Firebase's secure infrastructure

Third-Party Services

AllerAlert integrates with the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication and data storage
  • Tomorrow.io: Weather forecast data
  • Hugging Face: NLP processing for symptom extraction

Each third-party service has its own privacy policy governing the use of your information. We encourage you to review their policies.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • To comply with legal requirements or respond to lawful requests
  • To protect our rights, privacy, safety, or property
  • In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets (with prior notice)

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw Consent: Opt out of data collection at any time

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

Children's Privacy

AllerAlert is not intended for use by children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
