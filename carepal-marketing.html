About CarePal

Cirrhosis care at your fingertips

Your Liver Care in One Place

CarePal helps people living with cirrhosis and other liver conditions keep track of their care.

  • See your medications and set daily reminders
  • See your upcoming and past appointments
  • View your lab results and body measurements
  • Complete health surveys about how you are feeling
  • Ask Vik, an automated assistant, questions about liver health and your own records
  • Find educational resources about cirrhosis

Important

CarePal does not replace your care team and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. In an emergency, call 911.
