Account Deletion Request

Remove your CarePal account and data

⚠️

This action is permanent

Deleting your account permanently removes your CarePal data from our servers and cannot be undone.

What Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

Your account and profile information
Your chats with Vik
Your survey answers and in-progress surveys
Health readings you entered yourself
Safety alerts created from your chats

What Will Not Be Deleted

  • Your medical records: these belong to your care provider and are not changed.
  • Research study answers: answers already sent to a study database may be kept as required by that study's approved protocol.
  • Security record: a record of when this account was connected to your health record and which record it was.

Request Account Deletion

The quickest way is in the app: go to Profile and tap Delete My Account. It happens right away and also clears your reminders and saved answers from that phone. If you can no longer sign in, fill out this form using the email address you sign in with. It opens an email to our team, which you then need to send. We will process your request within 30 days and confirm by email.
