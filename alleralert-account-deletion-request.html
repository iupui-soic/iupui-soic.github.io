This action is permanent

Deleting your account will permanently remove all your data from our servers. This action cannot be undone.

What Will Be Deleted

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed from our systems:

👤 Your profile information and account credentials
📝 All symptom diary entries and health logs
🌡️ Environmental monitoring history and data
📊 Trend analysis and personalized insights
🔔 Notification preferences and settings
📍 Location history and risk zone data

Before You Go

Consider these alternatives before deleting your account:

  • Export your data: You can request a copy of all your health data before deletion
  • Pause notifications: If you're receiving too many alerts, adjust your notification settings in the app
  • Contact support: If you're experiencing issues, our team may be able to help resolve them
