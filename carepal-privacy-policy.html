Privacy Policy

How CarePal handles your information

Last Updated: October 31, 2026

Introduction

CarePal ("we," "our," or "us") is a mobile health application that helps people living with cirrhosis and other liver conditions keep track of their care. This Privacy Policy explains what information CarePal collects, how it is used and shared, and the choices you have.

By using CarePal, you agree to the collection and use of information as described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, profile photo, and a unique account identifier.

Your Health Records

When your account is connected to a health record, CarePal reads your demographics, medications, appointments and visits, lab results and other measurements, diagnoses, reports, and care team details from a health records server. This information is displayed in the app and used to answer your questions to Vik. Records stay on that server, and the app does not save a separate copy of them in your CarePal account. However, Vik's saved replies can include details from your records.

Information You Provide

  • Survey answers: your answers to health surveys, including questions about physical function, mood, anxiety, sleep, itching, and pain
  • Chat messages: the messages you send to Vik and the replies you receive
  • Health readings: any readings you choose to enter yourself
  • Medication reminders: the medicine names and times you set, which are stored only on your device

Safety Alerts

CarePal automatically checks chat messages for words that may suggest distress or a crisis. When it finds them, it saves an alert in your account containing the matching words, up to the first 200 characters of your message, the date, and a list of when such words appeared in your chats during the past 30 days. These alerts are not monitored, and no one is notified in real time. Do not rely on CarePal in an emergency.

Device Verification

CarePal uses Firebase App Check, with Google Play Integrity on Android and Apple DeviceCheck on iOS, to confirm that requests come from a genuine copy of the app.

What We Do Not Collect

CarePal does not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools, and it does not collect your location or access your contacts, camera, or microphone.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Show you your health information, appointments, and medications
  • Send the medication and survey reminders you set up or start
  • Save your chats and in-progress surveys so that you can return to them
  • Answer your questions through Vik
  • Keep the service secure and working correctly

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

Vik, Our Automated Assistant

Important: Vik is an automated assistant, not a doctor. To answer you, your messages and related parts of your health records are sent to Groq, a third-party AI service. Do not include information in your messages that you do not want to share with this service.

Data Storage and Security

  • Firebase Authentication: manages your sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore: stores your chats, surveys, readings, and safety alerts in the United States, in an area of the database restricted to your account
  • In transit: data sent between the app and our services uses encrypted connections
  • Technical logs: our server functions keep technical logs in Google Cloud for troubleshooting, and these can include error details
  • On your device: reminders, in-progress survey answers, and recently viewed account data are kept in the app's private storage and are not separately encrypted by CarePal, so please protect your phone with a passcode

No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

We keep your chats, surveys, readings, safety alerts, and profile for as long as your CarePal account exists, so that you can return to them.

When you ask us to delete your account through our account deletion request page, we delete your account and the data linked to it from our systems within 30 days. Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device, but it does not delete your account. Answers that have already been sent to a research study database may be kept as required by that study's approved protocol.

Third-Party Services

CarePal relies on the following services to operate:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, data storage, server functions, and device verification
  • Google Sign-In: signing in to the app
  • Groq: generating Vik's replies, as described above
  • REDCap, hosted by Indiana University: survey questions, and survey answers when submission to a study is enabled
  • Apple and Google notification services on your device: showing your reminders

Each service has its own privacy policy. Educational links in the app open websites, including YouTube, that have their own privacy practices.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share it only as described in this policy and in these circumstances:

  • With your consent, including the consent you give to take part in a research study
  • To comply with legal requirements or respond to lawful requests
  • To protect the rights, safety, or property of you or others

Your Rights

You can ask us to:

  • Access: give you a copy of your CarePal data
  • Correction: correct inaccurate information. Corrections to your medical records must be made with your care provider.
  • Deletion: delete your account through our account deletion request page

You can also remove a reminder at any time, and turn off notifications in your device settings.

Children's Privacy

CarePal is intended for adults. It is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date.
